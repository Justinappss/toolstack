# I Lost YouTube Monetization After Watching My Own Video

**Published:** November 2, 2026  
**Author:** Justin Pirrie, Founder of ToolStack  
**URL:** https://toolstack.tech/blog/lost-youtube-monetization-adsense-invalid-traffic

On Saturday, October 10, 2026, Google disabled the AdSense account behind my YouTube channel (Forgotten Towns, 28,864 subscribers) for "invalid traffic". On October 13 it rejected my appeal: "we are unable to reinstate your publisher account… You are not eligible for further participation in our publisher programs, and may not create new accounts."

The only unusual thing I did: watched my own new video on my own network the night before. I don't know whether I clicked an ad. Google does not disclose what it detected.

## What the analytics show
5,403,783 views over 365 days, 8:16 average view duration, 95.3% US, mostly Browse features traffic.

## How to protect your AdSense account
Never click your own ads, brief your household, turn off autoplay into your own channel, watch Studio for traffic oddities, and keep a second income stream.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can watching your own video cause invalid traffic?**
Watching is not a violation, clicking ads on your own content is. The cause in my case is unproven.

**Q: Can I open a new AdSense account after being disabled?**
Google's email said I may not create new accounts.
